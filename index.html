FAQ

Good to know

Does permanent makeup hurt?

Most clients describe it as mild scratching. A topical numbing cream is applied before and during treatment for comfort.

How long do results last?

Brows usually last 1 to 3 years and lip blush 2 to 3 years, depending on skin type, lifestyle and sun exposure. A yearly colour refresh keeps them fresh.

Why is a touch-up included?

Skin heals differently for everyone. The touch-up at around 6 weeks perfects shape and colour so your healed result is just right.

Who is not suitable for PMU?

PMU is not done during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with certain medical conditions or medications. We go through everything during your free consult.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

PMU appointments are secured with a deposit that goes toward your treatment. Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before.
